🎮 How to Play

🕵️ Your Mission

Find Ginger by exploring every location and collecting clues.

🔍 Investigate Everything

Click on objects that seem suspicious. Some objects hide important clues, while others are just distractions.

📜 Read Every Clue

Open notes and messages carefully. Each clue helps you figure out where to go next.

🗺️ Follow the Trail

Every important clue points to another location. Think like a detective and decide where Ginger went.

🐾 Find Ginger

Collect all the important clues and discover Ginger's final location.


Good luck, Detective!

💡 Tips

Click on everything—you never know what might be useful.
Not every object contains a clue.
Read every message carefully before moving on.
If you're stuck, think about the last clue you found.
